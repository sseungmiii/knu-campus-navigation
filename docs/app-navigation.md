# Map and timetable navigation

The Next shell owns five persistent bottom tabs: map, timetable, campus interior, school surroundings, and route surroundings. The timetable is a dedicated non-modal page, leaving the navigation available. Its hidden underlying iframe retains routes and map state. The map tab shows at most a 48px class summary; detailed comparisons and management live on the timetable page. Navigating to a class or choosing a place destination returns to the map. The map's same-origin `shell=1` mode hides its legacy bottom bar and reclaims the panel's former bottom inset.

`knu:shell-tab` and `knu:shell-map` use the existing validated same-origin/source bridge. Tab requests do not carry location or credentials. School surroundings searches fixed campus coordinates within 2km through the Kakao SDK, across restaurant/cafe/convenience categories. Campus-address matches are excluded, IDs deduplicated, partial failures disclosed, and late responses ignored. Kakao search pagination caps apply; this is a discovery list rather than an exhaustive directory. Existing route-surroundings functionality is retained separately.

Recurring meetings share a subject card when their normalized title, term and semester dates match. The stored meeting IDs, rooms, start/end times and favorite routes remain distinct. Existing rows need no migration. Photo review groups repeated subjects and edits their shared name/professor together. Manual entry still saves a two-meeting registration in one INSERT. Users can manage all subjects or filter by weekday.

Group editing validates every supplied meeting, verifies every ID belongs to the authenticated user, then makes one RLS-scoped bulk upsert. Caller-supplied owners/favorites are ignored; existing favorites are preserved. Group deletion similarly checks all IDs before one owner-filtered deletion. Single-meeting updates and deletion continue to work. Tests cover unknown IDs, ownership spoofing, favorite preservation, invalid-batch rejection, recurring groups and campus/outside separation.
